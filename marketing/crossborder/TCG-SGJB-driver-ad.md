**Title:** SG ⇄ JB Cross-Border Van & Lorry Drivers – Register Interest (TCG Express)

TCG Express is launching Singapore ⇄ Johor Bahru cross-border deliveries for tech and electronics companies – IT equipment, servers, spare parts and office moves. We are building a small team of verified driver partners for this lane.

WHAT THE JOBS LOOK LIKE
- Same-day runs between Singapore and Johor (Iskandar Puteri, Senai/Kulai, Pasir Gudang, JB city)
- A fixed fare per trip, shown upfront in the app – tap Accept to take the job
- Customs paperwork (Singapore export permit and Malaysian import declaration) is arranged before pickup – you carry the documents and drive

WHAT YOU NEED
- Your own Singapore-registered van or lorry with a valid Malaysia VEP (RFID tag) and Touch 'n Go
- Motor insurance that covers West Malaysia and goods carried for hire and reward
- A valid passport (at least 6 months left) and the right licence for your vehicle
- The right to work in Singapore as a self-employed driver partner
- Cross-border experience through Woodlands or Tuas checkpoint is a plus

HOW TO REGISTER INTEREST
Search "TCG Express" on the App Store or Google, sign up as a driver, then message us here with your vehicle type, whether your VEP is active, and how often you cross. New drivers pay 0% commission for their first 30 days.

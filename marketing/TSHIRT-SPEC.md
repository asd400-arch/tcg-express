## Critical requirement — QR code

The back print carries a QR code. It must be **scannable by a phone after printing and after washing**.

- Keep the white quiet zone around the code exactly as drawn — do not crop or resize it independently
- Print the code at full black-on-white contrast; do not screen or halftone it
- Please confirm the print method you will use and that it holds QR edge definition
